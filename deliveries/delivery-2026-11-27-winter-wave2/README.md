# Approved Winter Wave 2 artwork delivery

This historical art delivery installs the approved original Frostbloom Tree, Snow Lantern Circle and Arctic Fox PNGs unchanged. Its original changes.json records two disabled supported registrations and their default sprites; Arctic Fox is installed in the Animal pilot folder. Native sizes are 32×48 for Frostbloom/Fox and 64×96 for the 2×2 lantern circle.

The [November 28 release-settings delivery](../delivery-2026-11-28-release-approvals/README.md) and [approval record](../../handoffs/forest-release-2026/approval.json) supersede the original business placeholders for **Frostbloom and Snow Lantern Circle only**. They now have approved ordinary seasonal-store acquisition, December 1–February 28 purchase/every-new-placement windows, and prices of 3 unlock/1 placement and 8 unlock/4 placement respectively. Both remain disabled pending native verification. No art bytes or revisions change.

Arctic Fox remains an Animal pilot with unresolved Familiars, exact dates, acquisition and prices. Its proposed Winter window is not approved by the supported-object decision. The [current manifest](../../pilots/winter_objects_wave2/manifest.json) and [Xcode handoff](../../pilots/winter_objects_wave2/XCODE_HANDOFF.md) distinguish these scopes. Existing ownership, saved forests and unrelated content settings remain unchanged.
